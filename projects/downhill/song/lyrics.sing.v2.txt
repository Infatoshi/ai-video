[Intro - solo piano, soft brushes, waltz]

[Verse 1]
Two spirals of dots, one blue and one black,
Wound round each other and circling back,
Draw me a line that keeps them apart,
The machine can't do it, not at the start

[Instrumental - piano]

[Verse 2]
Inside the machine there's a row of knobs,
Three hundred thirty-seven, we call them weights,
Turn any knob and the line starts to bend,
They start out random, the line's in the wrong place

[Instrumental - piano]

[Verse 3]
So measure how wrong it is, dot after dot,
Add it up to one number, and that is the loss,
Every setting of the knobs is a place on a map,
The loss is how high, and we want to go down

[Instrumental - piano]

[Chorus]
Measure how wrong it is,
Find which way is down,
Take one small step that way,
And do it again, and again

[Instrumental - piano]

[Verse 4]
It can't see the valley, it can't see the ground,
It feels for the slope right under its feet,
Which way is down, that's the gradient,
How big a step, that's the learning rate

[Instrumental - piano]

[Chorus]
Measure how wrong it is,
Find which way is down,
Take one small step that way,
And do it again, and again

[Bridge]
Now make every step ten times as big,
It jumps right over the valley floor,
Lands on the far side, higher than before,
It bounces and bounces and never gets down

[Instrumental - piano]

[Chorus]
Measure how wrong it is,
Find which way is down,
Take one small step that way,
And do it again, and again

[Outro]
Three thousand one hundred twenty-one steps,
And every dot is on its own side,
Next time: why it never sees a letter

[End - piano]
